Bun Do

One place to edit a task.

Three ways to replace the scattered task menus. Same task, same capabilities, different editing flow.

Try this

  1. Open Edit. Change the title and a step.
  2. Set a due date and a weekly repeat.
  3. Save, complete a step, then reopen it.
  4. Try typed capture and a simulated voice draft.

Browser study, not the Android app. Sample household only. Voice, AI, sharing and sync are simulated. Reload resets the data.

What to evaluate

Can you find every edit without guessing a menu? Does Save mean what you expect? Is a guided flow helpful or just extra tapping?

Repeat plans affect future copies. Completing and claiming remain immediate actions outside the editor.
