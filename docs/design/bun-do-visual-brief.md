# Bun Do visual brief

The owner approved the refined rabbit salute on September 12, 2026. Current
vectors, exports, and provenance live in `assets/brand/`. `DESIGN.md` and the
[screen contracts](screen-contracts.md) own the selected visual direction.

Bun Do means "the way of the bun." Its identity is an original small rabbit
with composed martial-arts character: dry humor, recognizable silhouette, and
no copied King Bun artwork. The mascot may add warmth to empty states, recording,
and completion feedback. It must never hide recording state, replace literal
task actions, create member rankings, or turn missed work into shame.

The queue is an operational screen for two people sharing household work. Favor
readability, fast capture, accessible controls, and clear local-versus-shared
status. Rendered Android review remains implementation work; this brief is not
proof of a completed design system or device validation.
