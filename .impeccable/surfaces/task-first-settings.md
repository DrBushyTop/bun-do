# Task-first settings and secondary screens

Mode: Operate. The owner selected option B on September 17, 2026. Preserve the
native notebook palette and artwork. DESIGN.md owns the shared direction.

Navigation rows have a full-width touch target, left-aligned label and trailing
chevron. Values wrap below labels. Toggles and choices expose one semantic
control per row. Help disclosures announce whether they are expanded.
Use Material buttons for actions, not clickable text masquerading as headings.

Place tasks and choices before explanations. Privacy destinations, unsynced
work and destructive consequences remain visible at their decisions. Use
explicit separate recovery, sign-out and local-deletion confirmations.

Phone layouts scroll naturally at large text sizes. No fixed label heights,
shrunk type, or clipped action labels. Verify Finnish and English in native
Android, including empty/loading/error states and system Back. Browser study
captures are not evidence for the native implementation.
